05 / DELIVER

Export MP4

LOCAL DOWNLOAD

Export selected clips with their saved format and crop settings. Encoding runs in this browser; files download to your device. No video is uploaded.

Output resolution is saved for each clip. For Original framing, 720p and 1080p fit within a matching landscape or portrait box without changing the aspect ratio.

First export requires downloading the FFmpeg engine (~32 MB) from your site. This is a single-threaded build. For initial testing, use a short H.264 MP4; long or 4K videos can exceed browser memory.
